**Domain Complexity and Engineering Context**

The principal engineering challenge is not merely transferring a payment request between systems. A cross-border banking workflow may require different handling depending on **the destination country, the bank's legal or operational entity, whether the institution is a local bank or an overseas branch, the available local payment infrastructure, and the format of account or payment identifiers**. These dimensions can interact, creating cases in which apparently similar corporate transactions require different validation or processing behavior.

Within this domain, backend implementation must represent the underlying banking rules clearly, reject or identify incompatible inputs appropriately, and avoid treating country-specific assumptions as universal. Effective delivery therefore requires careful requirements analysis, collaboration across technical and business functions, and precise treatment of transaction correctness.
